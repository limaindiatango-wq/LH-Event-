Frequently asked questions

Can I register for more than one session?

Yes — submit the form again with a different session selected. Each submission is tracked as a separate registration in the attendee list.

What happens if a session is full?

You're automatically placed on that session's waitlist instead of being blocked. If a seat opens up, an organizer can move you to Registered from the attendee list.

Will I receive a real confirmation email?

Not in this demo. It shows exactly what the confirmation email would contain, but no message is actually sent since there's no backend or email service connected yet.

Is my data saved anywhere?

No — everything lives in memory in your browser tab and resets the moment you reload or close the page.
